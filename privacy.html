Bujhau Uploader — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026
Operator: बुझौं (Bujhau Nepali, YouTube @BujhauNepali), an independent Nepali-language education channel based in Japan.
Contact: bujhaunnepali@gmail.com

1. What this tool is

Bujhau Uploader is a small, private tool that runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It is used by the owner of the Bujhau channel to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not have other users.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

Bujhau Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2b. Use of TikTok APIs

Bujhau Uploader also uses TikTok for Developers (Login Kit and Content Posting API) to post the channel owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. With your permission (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish) it reads only your TikTok display name and avatar to show which account is connected, and uploads the videos you choose with the caption and privacy setting you choose. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy. The TikTok access token is stored only on the owner's computer and is deleted when you click "Disconnect TikTok"; you can also remove access in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

3. Information we access, collect, store and share

  • Accessed: with your permission (OAuth scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl), the tool uploads videos, sets thumbnails and adds subtitles on the YouTube channel you authorize. It reads back only the ID and status of the videos it uploads.
  • Stored: the OAuth access/refresh token is stored only in a local file (token.json) on the owner's computer. Video files, titles, descriptions and subtitles stay on that computer; the tool keeps no database or server copy.
  • Collected / shared: no personal data is collected, sold, or shared with any third party. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube to perform the upload you request. No analytics, advertising or cookies are used.
  • Retention: the tool does not keep YouTube API data beyond the video IDs written into the owner's own upload notes.

4. How to revoke access and delete data

You can revoke Bujhau Uploader's access at any time:

  • Click "Revoke access" in the tool — this revokes the token with Google and deletes the local token.json; or
  • Visit the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

After access is revoked, all stored authorization data is deleted. To request deletion of anything else, email bujhaunnepali@gmail.com; we will respond within 7 days.

5. Children

The tool is not directed to children and is used only by the adult channel owner.

6. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions or complaints about these privacy practices: bujhaunnepali@gmail.com.
